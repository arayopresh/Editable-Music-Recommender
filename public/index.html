Taste profile

See what an AI believes about your music taste, and correct it.

What the AI can and cannot do

The AI can

  • Read your listening history and write your taste profile
  • Recommend songs and show which line each came from

The AI cannot

  • Change or remove a line you wrote or edited
  • Add anything to your profile without your permission

It can get your taste wrong so if a line is wrong, edit it and the tracks change. Every song is checked on Deezer in case the AI hallucinaates.
